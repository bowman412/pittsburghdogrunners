Good questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can any dog go on a run?

Not every dog is suited to running. Age, health, fitness, weather, and individual temperament all matter. We'll discuss your dog before arranging a session. Consult your veterinarian if you're unsure whether running is appropriate.

Where do you operate?

We focus on Pittsburgh and surrounding neighborhoods. Use the booking process to share your location and confirm service availability.

How do I book a run?

Click any Book a Run button to visit our booking platform. Booking details and availability will depend on our configured customer portal.

What if my dog is new to running?

Let us know about your dog's experience and energy level. Running should be introduced gradually and be appropriate for the individual dog.

Good questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can any dog go on a run?

Not every dog is suited to running. Age, breed, health, fitness, weather, and individual temperament all matter. We'll discuss your dog before arranging a session. Consult your veterinarian if you're unsure whether running is appropriate.

Where do you operate?

We focus on Pittsburgh and surrounding neighborhoods. Contact us with your location to confirm availability.

How do I book a run?

Contact us to discuss your dog and scheduling. Online booking details will be added here when available.

What if my dog is new to running?

Tell us about your dog's experience and energy level. Any introduction to running should be gradual and appropriate for the individual dog.
